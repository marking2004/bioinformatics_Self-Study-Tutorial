生物信息学，从零开始自学

Bioinformatics, self-taught from scratch

我是高校生物信息学课程的任课教师。这里把课堂内容整理成可自学的图文教程、命令示例与参考资料， 方便本科生课前预习、课后复习、动手练习——少一点黑话，多一点能直接敲的命令。全站提供中英双语。

I teach an undergraduate bioinformatics course. Here the lectures are reorganized into illustrated, hands-on tutorials with runnable commands and references — for preview, review and practice. Less jargon, more commands you can actually type. The whole site is bilingual (中文 / English).
